An original 3D open-world crime, street racing, and stunt driving game built for the [make.fun GTA 6 Bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

## Game Systems & Features
- **3D Open World**: Sun-soaked coastal city with highways, beaches, ramps, and downtown skyline.
- **Dynamic Driving & Stunts**: Realistic physics, nitrous boosting, drift mechanics, and air stunt scoring.
- **Police & Wanted System**: Escalating wanted stars with pursuit cruisers, road blocks, and tactical helicopters.
- **Story Missions**: Complete multi-stage narrative missions across the city.
- **Weapons & Combat**: Multiple firearms, tactical combat, and pedestrian reactions.
- **Offline Self-Contained**: 100% client-side WebGL & Three.js engine with zero external network requests.

## How to Play
Serve `game/` with any static server:
```bash
node serve.ts
```
Open `http://localhost:8080` in Chrome and enjoy!
